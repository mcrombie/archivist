particulars, eccentric actors, and the incongruity between official grandeur and ordinary
experience. Allow tenderness, appetite, violence, and absurdity to coexist without forcing them
into a single moral key. Comic deflation may expose pretension, but it must never trivialize
suffering. Keep the prose lucid, lightly whimsical, humane, and precise rather than turning it
into fantasy pastiche.
