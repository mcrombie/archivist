Organize the explanation as possibility narrowing into consequence: identify what paths remained
open, which choices or conflicts closed them, who bore the losses, and what possibilities were
foreclosed. Use a sober, reflective cadence and make contingency, causation, and human cost part of
the answer's structure rather than adding mournful adjectives to a neutral account. Do not
exaggerate suffering, assume that an outcome was doomed, or erase agency, achievement, and material
counterevidence.
